Refund policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

1. Free Basic plan

The Basic plan (market analysis and recommendations) is free, so no payment or refund applies.

2. Monthly plans

Standard, Pro and Premium plans are billed monthly in advance. Payments for a month in which work has already started are non-refundable. If you decide to stop a plan, email us before your next billing date and no further charges will be made; the work for the already paid month is completed.

3. Custom projects

For websites and smartphone apps, payment terms and any refunds are set out in the written agreement for each project. Payments for work already delivered are non-refundable.

4. Advertising spend

Ad spend paid to third-party platforms (such as Instagram or Pinterest) is governed by those platforms and cannot be refunded by Bizgrowly.

5. Billing errors

If you believe you were charged in error, contact us within 30 days of the charge and we will review it and correct any mistake.

6. Contact us

For refund questions, please contact Bizgrowly at hello@bizgrowly.com.
