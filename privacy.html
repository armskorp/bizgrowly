Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Bizgrowly, a digital marketing agency offering website and app development, Instagram and Pinterest growth, and Google Business Profile management, collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. When you use our contact form, we collect your name, business name, email address and the content of your message. Submissions are processed and delivered to us by our form provider, Web3Forms.

Technical information. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. When you visit, these providers may automatically process basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, the pages requested and the date and time of the request, for example in server logs. We do not use this data to identify you.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide information about our services.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you request.
  • To keep our website secure, reliable and working properly.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases for processing

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR apply, we process personal information on the following bases: consent, when you choose to contact us; steps prior to or performance of a contract, when you ask about or use our services; legitimate interests, such as operating and securing our website and replying to business inquiries; and legal obligation, where the law requires it.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes. We share information only with trusted service providers that help us operate our website and business, such as our hosting provider (GitHub), font provider (Google), analytics provider (Google Analytics), contact form provider (Web3Forms) and email provider, and only as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

5. Data retention

We keep contact form submissions and related correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you and manage our business relationship, generally no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless a longer period is required by law. Technical logs are retained by our hosting and font providers according to their own policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, including the GDPR and the CCPA, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent at any time.
  • Receive a copy of your information in a portable format.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your rights.

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@bizgrowly.com. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

7. Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website in aggregate. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga) and collects information such as the pages you visit, your device and browser type, and your approximate location derived from your IP address. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics using Google's opt-out browser add-on: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The site always opens in English. The language you choose applies only to the current visit and is not stored. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address and browser information; please see Google's privacy policy for details.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

We work with clients around the world, and our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, to protect your information.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, including encrypted HTTPS connections. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please contact Bizgrowly at hello@bizgrowly.com.
